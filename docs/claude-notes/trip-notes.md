# Mae Hong Son motorcycle loop (future trip, dates open)

It started in another AI tool as a two-page brochure; this repo replaces it. Moved here from `~/Documents/Claude/thailand-trip-2026/motorcycle-loop/` on Nov 21, 2026.

## Decided (Nov 20, 2026)
- Group of 3 or more (exact number open); one mid-size adventure bike each (CB500X class).
- Lodging alternates between comfortable and budget; the per-night picks are in the page's budget calculator.
- Dates open; aim for Nov to Feb. Avoid Yi Peng (about Nov 24 and 25) and New Year week. Late Nov to early Dec catches the Mae U-Kho sunflowers.
- **8-day "middle" plan (chosen Nov 20, 2026):** the group rides fast and doesn't need rest days. Days: 1 arrive Chiang Mai; 2 Doi Suthep and Samoeng (~140 km); 3 to Pai (~135); 4 Pai to Mae Hong Son by Tham Lod, Ban Rak Thai, and Pang Ung (~185); 5 to Khun Yuam with Su Tong Pae, the museum, and Mae U-Kho (~145); 6 Khun Yuam to Mae Klang Luang by Mae Sariang and Ob Luang (~260, the long day); 7 Inthanon summit at sunrise and back to Chiang Mai (~130); 8 fly out. 7 nights, 6 bike days, about 1,000 km.
- Dropped from the 11-day version: the Mae Hong Son rest day, the Mae Sariang overnight, and the swifts at Tham Lod at dusk. A 6-day fast version (sleeping in Mae Sariang instead of Khun Yuam, and doing Samoeng on Day 1 afternoon) was the alternative.
- Daylight rule: off mountain roads by about 5:45 pm; Days 4 and 6 need a 7:00 to 7:30 start.

## Fixes to the old brochure
- Its distance column labeled kilometers as miles. The loop is about 600 km (375 mi); with side trips it's about 975 km.
- The Khun Yuam museum covers Japanese soldiers in WWII, not the Death Railway.

## Budget (default: 4 people, 4 bikes, 2 rooms, mixed food)
8-day plan: about 31,900 THB, or $948 per person, at 33.7 THB/USD (includes flights from Bangkok to Chiang Mai and back, plus 10% contingency). Not included: the bike deposit (10,000 to 20,000 THB, refundable), travel insurance, and international flights.
