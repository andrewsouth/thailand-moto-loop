Route highlights

Eleven stops

Start and finish

Chiang Mai

The old Lanna capital: a moated city of temples, markets, and northern food.

Day 2

Doi Suthep

The gilded chedi of Wat Phra That Doi Suthep, high above the city.

Day 3

Huai Nam Dang

Cool pine forest and a sea of mist filling the valleys at dawn.

Day 3

Pai

A laid-back valley town with hot springs, cafés, and the narrow ridges of Pai Canyon.

Day 4

Tham Lod Cave

A river cave with ancient teak coffins, explored by lantern and bamboo raft.

Day 4

Ban Rak Thai and Pang Ung

A Yunnanese tea village on the border, and a pine-ringed reservoir in morning mist.

Days 4 and 5

Mae Hong Son

A Shan mountain town of lakeside temples and the white chedis of Doi Kong Mu.

Day 5

Khun Yuam and Doi Mae U-Kho

Wartime history in town, and hillsides of wild sunflowers from late November.

Day 6

Ob Luang

A narrow granite gorge, crossed by a footbridge above the rapids.

Day 6

Mae Klang Luang

A Karen village of rice terraces and mountain coffee on the slopes of Doi Inthanon.

Days 6 and 7

Doi Inthanon

Thailand's highest mountain, at 2,565 m (8,415 ft): cloud forest, waterfalls, and the twin royal chedis looking out over the ranges we rode.

The Mae Hong Son Loop and beyond

Eight days, one big loop, and some of the best riding roads in Asia. Starts and finishes in Chiang Mai, November to February.
